# HydraSafe Customer Acquisition Environment

Status: ACTIVE
Owner: DiamondOps / HydraSafe
Created: 2026-08-02

## Objective
Convert qualified CVD diamond growers and diamond-reactor OEMs into paid HydraSafe remediation engagements through a bounded, no-cost review of existing hydrogen permit and safety documentation.

## Target market
Primary geography:
- United States
- Surat, Gujarat, India

Primary segments:
1. CVD diamond growers and manufacturers operating hydrogen-bearing reactors.
2. Diamond CVD reactor OEMs and process-platform suppliers whose customers face hydrogen installation, commissioning, permitting, and operating-documentation requirements.

## Offer boundary
The no-cost assessment:
- reviews only documentation already supplied by the prospect;
- compares the supplied set against the HydraSafe intake checklist;
- returns a concise gap register;
- classifies each item as `present`, `missing`, `inconsistent`, `outdated`, or `specialist-review-required`;
- does not certify compliance, approve engineering, rewrite permits, or replace legal, fire-code, process-safety, or licensed engineering review.

Paid remediation may include document normalization, evidence indexing, missing-document work packages, authority-facing submission preparation, change tracking, and implementation support under a separately approved scope.

## Files
- `prospects.csv` — research-backed acquisition registry.
- `outreach.md` — email sequence, qualification questions, and conversion rules.
- `assessment-intake.md` — bounded free-assessment intake and scoring structure.

## Prospect workflow
Use these stages exactly:

`RESEARCHED -> VERIFIED -> READY_FOR_OUTREACH -> CONTACTED -> REPLIED -> INTAKE_REQUESTED -> ASSESSMENT_ACTIVE -> GAP_REPORT_DELIVERED -> PAID_SCOPE_PROPOSED -> WON | LOST | NURTURE`

A record may not advance to `VERIFIED` unless its organization, location, segment, CVD relevance, and public contact path have been checked against a current public source.

## Priority scoring
Score each prospect from 0–10:
- +3 confirmed CVD diamond growth or diamond CVD reactor activity;
- +2 explicit hydrogen/plasma/vacuum process relevance;
- +2 United States or Surat location;
- +1 visible production scale, expansion, or commercial reactor deployment;
- +1 public business contact route;
- +1 apparent compliance or installation complexity.

Priority bands:
- 8–10: Tier A
- 5–7: Tier B
- 0–4: Tier C / research only

## Operating cadence
1. Verify five records per working session.
2. Move no more than ten verified records into an outreach batch.
3. Personalize the first line using only public, source-supported facts.
4. Log every contact date, response, next action, and disposition in `prospects.csv`.
5. Do not claim regulatory approval, guaranteed compliance, or certification.
6. Review and refresh stale records after 90 days.

## Success criteria
The environment is activation-ready when:
- 25–40 prospects are installed;
- at least 20 are verified;
- at least 10 are Tier A or Tier B with a public contact route;
- the offer, intake, and conversion boundaries are installed;
- the handoff reflects current state and next action.
