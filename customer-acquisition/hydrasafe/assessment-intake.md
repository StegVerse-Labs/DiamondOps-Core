## Eligibility gate
The free review proceeds only when:
- the prospect confirms a hydrogen-bearing CVD diamond process or directly supported installation;
- a responsible business contact authorizes document review;
- documents are supplied by the prospect through an approved channel;
- the prospect accepts that the output is a gap assessment, not certification or approval.

Stop and refer for specialist review when the request requires engineering design, code interpretation, legal advice, incident response, emergency instruction, or a licensed seal.

## Document checklist
Record one status per item: `PRESENT`, `MISSING`, `INCONSISTENT`, `OUTDATED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `SPECIALIST_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

### System identity and authority
- Facility and reactor identification
- Process description and operating envelope
- Equipment manuals and current revision list
- Applicable authority and permit register
- Named document owners and approval authority

### Hydrogen supply and distribution
- Supply source and maximum inventory
- Cylinder, bundle, bulk, or generator documentation
- Piping and instrumentation diagram
- Component/material compatibility records
- Regulator, valve, manifold, and pressure-rating records
- Isolation and lockout points
- Purge and inerting procedure

### Detection, ventilation, and shutdown
- Hydrogen detection layout and setpoints
- Alarm logic and notification path
- Ventilation design basis and operating verification
- Interlock and emergency shutdown narrative
- Fail-safe state and loss-of-power behavior
- Functional-test and calibration records

### Installation and commissioning
- Site acceptance and commissioning record
- Leak and pressure-test evidence
- Electrical classification or specialist determination
- Exhaust, vent, and discharge routing record
- As-built drawings
- Punch-list closure evidence

### Operations and maintenance
- Startup, normal operation, and shutdown procedures
- Preventive-maintenance schedule
- Detector calibration and replacement schedule
- Change-control procedure
- Operator training and competency records
- Contractor and service-provider controls

### Emergency and incident readiness
- Emergency response plan
- Alarm and evacuation instructions
- Fire department or authority coordination record
- Emergency contact and escalation list
- Incident / near-miss reporting process
- Drill or exercise records

### Permit and external evidence
- Current permits and approval correspondence
- Inspection reports and open findings
- Insurer or landlord requirements
- Previous gap assessments
- Corrective-action register
- Modification history after last approval

## Gap register
For each gap capture:
- Gap ID
- Checklist item
- Status
- Evidence reviewed
- Why the item is flagged
- Responsible owner
- Specialist dependency
- Urgency: immediate / before operation / before modification / administrative
- Proposed paid-remediation work package

## Free deliverable
The no-cost output is limited to:
1. Assessment identity and scope.
2. Documents reviewed.
3. Checklist status totals.
4. A concise gap register.
5. Specialist dependencies and explicit exclusions.
6. Optional paid-remediation work packages.

## Quality controls
- Every conclusion must cite a supplied document or identify the absence of one.
- Do not infer that an undocumented control exists.
- Do not mark a document current without a date or revision basis.
- Preserve uncertainty explicitly.
- Separate factual document gaps from engineering judgments.
- Record the assessment cutoff date so later changes cannot be mistaken for reviewed state.
