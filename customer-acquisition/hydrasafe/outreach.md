## Positioning
HydraSafe offers a bounded, no-cost review of documentation already maintained for hydrogen-bearing CVD diamond systems. The review identifies missing, inconsistent, outdated, or specialist-review-required items. It is not a certification, permit approval, engineering seal, or legal opinion.

## Qualification questions
1. Does the site operate or support a diamond CVD system using hydrogen or a hydrogen-containing process mixture?
2. Is the request for an operating site, a new installation, a moved reactor, or an OEM customer handoff?
3. Which authority, insurer, landlord, or internal standard governs the current documentation set?
4. Are documents available for gas supply, ventilation, detection, shutdown, emergency response, commissioning, maintenance, and change control?
5. Has the system changed since the current permit or safety package was prepared?
6. Is there a deadline, inspection, commissioning date, expansion, or customer acceptance event?
7. Who owns decisions requiring a licensed engineer, fire-protection specialist, code consultant, or legal review?

## Conversion rule
A paid remediation scope may be proposed only after the free gap report is delivered and the prospect confirms which gaps it wants addressed. The proposal must separate:
- document assembly and normalization;
- evidence indexing and traceability;
- missing-document drafting;
- engineering or specialist dependencies;
- authority-facing submission support;
- implementation and verification support.

Never state or imply that HydraSafe guarantees permit issuance, compliance, inspection success, or authority acceptance.
