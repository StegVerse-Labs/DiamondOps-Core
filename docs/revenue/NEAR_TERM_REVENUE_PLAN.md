# StegVerse Near-Term Revenue Plan

Prepared: 2026-08-02
Status: ACTIVE

## Governing sequence
Do not audit the full repository portfolio before selling. Execute two parallel revenue tracks:

1. HydraSafe facility permitting-readiness engagements for near-term invoices.
2. Entity registration, SAM, VetCert, and one AaCT-E-centered DoD SBIR proposal for non-dilutive funding.

## Track A — HydraSafe permitting-readiness packets

### Product
A documentation-only, per-facility hydrogen and reactive-gas permitting-readiness packet assembled from DiamondOps components:
- DiamondOps-Core: shared contracts and liability boundary;
- HydraSafe: hazard-review, permit, commissioning, inspection, incident-response, and evidence-package frameworks;
- ReactorOps: reactor inventory, OEM document, workflow, commissioning, inspection, and procedure evidence inputs;
- YieldOS: validated-record aggregation, completeness reporting, review queues, and delivery receipts;
- CrystalWorks: excluded from the initial sales path unless a customer specifically requires experimental-program records.

### Commercial offer
- First two reference engagements: USD 8,000 each.
- Standard range after references: USD 8,000–25,000 per facility, scoped by reactor count, document condition, jurisdiction count, and external-review burden.
- Entry offer: bounded free gap assessment of customer-supplied documentation.
- Conversion event: customer accepts a remediation scope and statement of work.

### Delivery boundary
StegVerse assembles, structures, versions, reconciles, and reports documentation. It does not issue permits, certify compliance, authorize operation, or provide a professional engineering opinion. Required owner, OEM, insurer, AHJ, and licensed-professional reviews remain external.

### Binding dependency
Qualify at least one licensed fire-protection or process-safety PE willing to perform project-specific review and, where legally appropriate, stamp final submissions under a separate professional scope and fee.

### Activation gates
- 25–40 researched prospects installed and prioritized.
- First ten Tier A contacts verified.
- Free-assessment email sequence ready.
- PE partner brief ready and at least five candidates identified.
- Confidential intake and document-transfer method established.
- First accepted assessment converted into a signed SOW and deposit/invoice.

## Track B — non-dilutive funding

### Registration sequence
1. Form qualifying US for-profit entity.
2. Obtain EIN.
3. Complete SAM.gov entity registration and receive UEI.
4. Complete SBA Company Registry and obtain SBC Control ID.
5. Establish DSIP access and select one well-matched topic.
6. Submit one AaCT-E-centered Phase I proposal.

### Proposal positioning
Search and write in the language of autonomy assurance, runtime assurance, safety monitors, verifiable execution boundaries, and evidence-producing assurance—not generic governance.

AaCT-E should lead as a runnable evidence artifact whose claims can be checked by assertions and replayed. The proposal must connect the artifact to one solicitation-defined mission problem, measurable Phase I objectives, transition customer, work plan, and commercialization path.

### VetCert parallel path
After SAM credentials are active, prepare and submit VetCert using the entity ownership documents, DD-214, EIN evidence, and any documentation required for the requested VOSB or SDVOSB status. VetCert does not gate SBIR and must run in parallel.

## Repository ownership
- DiamondOps-Core owns this cross-repository plan and commercial contracts.
- HydraSafe owns the customer-facing offer, assessment, SOW, packet frameworks, and PE partnership workflow.
- ReactorOps owns reactor-documentation intake and evidence mapping.
- YieldOS owns packet aggregation, completeness views, and delivery receipts.
- CrystalWorks remains non-critical to near-term revenue.
- AaCT-E should own the runnable evidence package and SBIR technical volume when its connected repository becomes available.

## Immediate execution queue
1. Verify and prepare first ten HydraSafe accounts.
2. Begin PE partner qualification.
3. Establish entity/EIN/SAM registration record.
4. Create confidential customer intake and proposal/payment mechanics.
5. Identify the AaCT-E repository and install the SBIR technical-volume workspace there.
