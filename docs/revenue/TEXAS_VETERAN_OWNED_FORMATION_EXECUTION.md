## Form 205 data required before submission

For each LLC, complete without guessing:

- Article 1 — exact legal entity name.
- Article 2 — registered agent type/name and registered office.
- Article 3 — governing authority:
  - member-managed or manager-managed;
  - initial governing person(s);
  - governing person address(es) as required by the filing.
- Article 4 — purpose.
- Organizer name/address.
- Effectiveness of filing.
- Supplemental provisions, only if intentionally adopted.
- Execution/signature by authorized organizer.

## Form 05-904 data required before submission

- Entity name exactly matching Article 1.
- Printed name of every qualifying veteran owner.
- TVC unique identifier for each owner.
- Ownership percentage for each owner.
- Combined ownership = 100%.
- Required owner signature(s).

Private values belong in the private filing copy only; do not commit them here.

## Post-acceptance sequence

Only after direct evidence of SOS acceptance:

1. Record accepted legal name and formation date in the public tracker.
2. Obtain EIN through the authorized IRS path.
3. Establish banking/operating records as required.
4. Continue SAM.gov / UEI prerequisites when federal procurement requires them.
5. Continue SBA / DSIP / VetCert lanes as applicable.
6. Request the TVC-verified Veteran-Owned Business certificate/logo if desired after formation.
7. Do not claim federal registration, procurement eligibility, or governed activation without direct evidence.

## Current human-authority blocker
